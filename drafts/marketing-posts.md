Rule: these read as a person asking for input, not a launch. Reddit converts on honesty and burns astroturf.

### r/MealPrepSunday — `utm_campaign=mps-tool-question`
**Title:** I got tired of retyping TikTok recipes into my grocery list, so I'm building the missing pipe. Sanity-check me?
**Body:** Every Sunday I'd have 4 recipes saved across TikTok and Safari, then spend 40 minutes turning them into one deduped grocery list, then log it all again in a tracker by Wednesday and quit by Thursday.
