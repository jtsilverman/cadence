## Positioning — LOCKED 2026-08-12

**Thesis line:** Cadence fits the meal plan to your life, not your life to the
meal plan.

**The frame is follow-through, not weight loss and not dieting.** The buyer
wants to eat the way they intend to eat. The buyer fails at the moment the
routine breaks, then reads that break as personal failure and quits. Cadence
sells the recovery from the break.

### What changed and why

| Was | Is | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "The diet you actually follow." | "Take control of how you eat, without it taking control of your life." | "Diet" leaves the site entirely. It signals temporary and restrictive. |
| Six product beats | Five pains as direct-address questions | The visitor has to recognize the problem before a feature means anything. |
| Blame on the plan's rigidity, stated abstractly | Named moments (Friday night, the cheat meal, the rough night) | Specific beats abstract. |

### The GLP-1 position

**GLP-1 drugs are context, never a comparison.** No drug name appears in a
headline. The audience research settles the size question: only 22% of GLP-1
non-users want the drugs at all (KFF, Oct-Nov 2025), and 73% of people who want
to lose weight would rather change how they eat than inject (Morning Consult /
PCRM, Sept 2024, advocacy-funded, so trust the direction over the digit).

The whole category already converged on "alongside the medication" (Noom GLP-1
Companion, June 2026; MyFitnessPal GLP-1 Support, April 2026; WeightWatchers
prescribing). Being "the alternative" is the abandoned position, not novel
positioning.

**Compliance line, bright.** The FDA sent 30+ warning letters over drug-mimicry
marketing (Feb-Mar 2026). Safe: behavior claims ("plan your week", "hit your
protein target"). Unsafe: drug names in headlines, appetite or mechanism
language, before/after weight photos, "alternative to" beside a drug name.

**Where GLP-1 appears:** channel posts and the FAQ only. The off-ramp moment is
the honest angle worth writing (two-thirds of lost weight returns within a year
of stopping, STEP 1; 82% of the tirzepatide withdrawal arm regained over a
quarter of the loss, SURMOUNT-4). Source: `~/Documents/brain/sources/clippings/2026-08-11-glp1-positioning-research.md`.

## Voice rules — LOCKED

- **Never write "diet."** The word is retired from the site. Say "how you eat".
- **Never lead with "AI."** AI appears as *how*, never as the promise.
- **Always "Cadence," never "the coach."** The coach is never named and never
  personified.
- **No em dashes in site copy.**
- **Blame never lands on the reader.** This is the emotional spine.
- **One idea per headline.** Features are reasons to believe, not hooks.
- **Specific beats abstract.** Name the concrete moment.
- **Read every line at arm's length.** The audience skews older and less
  app-native than the TikTok-saver persona. Big type, few elements, no macro
  numbers anywhere in the demo.
- **The name is an argument.** A cadence is something you keep. The name may pay
  itself off, never explain itself.
- **No drug names in headlines.** See the compliance line above.

## Audience — LOCKED 2026-08-12

**The general audience is anyone who wants to track what they eat**, whether or
not they are consistent or good at it. The product makes planning, tracking, and
adjusting easier for all of them. It appeals hardest to the people who get off
track easily.

| Persona | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The Restarter** (primary) | Serial attempts. Quits after one bad week. | r/loseit, Monday and January windows |
| **The Meal-Prep Sunday-er** (secondary channel) | Already plans. Wants the overhead gone. | r/MealPrepSunday, ~5.7M members |
| Default Dinner Manager | Parents deciding dinner nightly. | Second wave |
| Social Eater | Eats out often, wants both. | Broad content |
| MFP Refugee | Logging fatigue. | Logging is the documented first behavior to lapse |
| GLP-1 Adjacent | On, off, or scared of the drug. | Channels and FAQ only |

### The science under the pains

Every pain traces to a documented mechanism, which is why the pain band is the
spine of the page.

- Diet logging is the first tracking behavior abandoned, median 10 weeks
  (Carpenter 2022).
- Logging drops Thursday through Sunday, and the gap widens over time
  (Pellegrini 2018).
- Reading one lapse as personal failure predicts worse outcomes (abstinence
  violation effect, Grilo 1992).
- Planned deviations cause no distress. Unplanned ones trigger the what-the-hell
  spiral (Ganson 2022).

**The product mechanism this justifies:** Cadence turns unplanned-feeling
deviations into planned-feeling ones. That is the claim the whole site makes.

## Superseded copy (2026-07-30)

Kept for the reasoning and the rejected directions. **Never copy from this

### Superseded hero

> The diet you actually follow.
> Not the one you tell yourself you'll start. Cadence builds your week around
> food you already like, then adapts when plans change, so you keep your goals
> and your Friday night.

**Why it worked at the time:** the headline conceded past failure without naming
a villain. "Actually" acknowledged that the reader had started and quit before,
without saying so.

**Why it went:** it leads with "diet", and the reposition retires the word.

**Rejected directions (do not relitigate):**

- *"Stop starting over on Monday."* Names the failure loop, matches the name's
  thesis. Held in reserve as an ad line.
- *"A plan that survives a Tuesday."* Leads with adaptation before the reader
  knows what the product is.
- *"Eating well, minus the spreadsheet."* Leads with friction removal, which is a
  feature, not the wedge.
- *"Meal prep you won't resent by Wednesday."* Scopes the product to meal prep
  alone.
- *"Make dieting less complicated."* Every competitor's claim.

### Superseded section 2

> You don't need more discipline. You need less overhead.
> The planning. The list. The guilt math after one night out. The Monday
> restart. Cadence takes care of all of it, so dieting stops being a second job
> and starts being second nature.

The headline **survives** the reposition and is now the pain band header. The
body is retired for the word "dieting" and for restating what the five pains
now carry.

**Rejected directions (do not relitigate):**

- *"Your training app doesn't know what's in your fridge."* Killed by the
  nutrition-only pivot.
- *"A food diary tells you what you did. Cadence tells you what's next."* Held
  in reserve if a comparison section gets built.
- *Anything referencing food quality.* The message is not about the food.
- *"admin"* as the word for the burden. Office-speak.
- *"It's not willpower. It's workload."* "Overhead" pairs better with the
  operations register.
